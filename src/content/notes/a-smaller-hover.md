A hover can make an interface easier to read without making every element feel animated.



This sample explores a short transition between a title and a preview. The row stays still, the preview appears nearby, and the pointer does not have to chase a moving target.



The effect should also work with a keyboard. On touch screens, opening the note directly is often clearer than introducing a second tap just to imitate a mouse.
