<p class="sample-disclosure">Sample note for evaluating the layout; not your authored writing.</p>

Every setting leaves a question for the next person who opens the app.



Imagine a note editor with separate controls for line height, paragraph spacing, page width, and text size. Each can be useful. Together they also turn a small writing task into a configuration exercise.



Before adding another control, try one good default and a realistic piece of content. A setting becomes more convincing when it solves a recurring problem that the default cannot.
