A small requirement change can reveal more about an application than a broad architectural diagram.



For this example, change a validation limit and list every place that needs attention. Include the calculation, its tests, and any explanation visible to the user.



If the list crosses several unrelated files, that is useful evidence. It gives the next design discussion a concrete starting point: which duplication serves a purpose, and which duplication simply makes a change harder?
