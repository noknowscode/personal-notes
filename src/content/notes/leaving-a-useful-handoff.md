The next session needs a place to begin, not a transcript of everything that happened.



A useful sample handoff records what works, what remains uncertain, and the next action that would reduce that uncertainty. A decision should carry its reason, particularly when a more obvious option was rejected.



Keep the note short enough to read before starting work. The details that matter are the ones that would otherwise have to be rediscovered.
